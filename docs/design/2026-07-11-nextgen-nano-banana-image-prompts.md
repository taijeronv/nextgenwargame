## Visual Contract

Use these rules across every generated asset:

- Editorial, operational, restrained, and evidence-focused.
- Inspired by premium architecture-editorial photography: strong geometry, negative space, natural material texture, controlled light.
- Palette: charcoal green `#17211d`, warm paper `#f2f0e9`, muted stone `#d8d2c3`, dark olive `#41533e`, restrained lime accent `#c8e36d`.
- Photorealistic materials: paper, matte card, pencil, acetate overlays, binder tabs, wood or dark composite tabletop.
- Military context should come from planning geometry, decision tools, controller materials, maps, and workflow artifacts—not weapons or spectacle.
- Leave deliberate negative space for website text overlays.
- No readable text inside generated images unless a prompt explicitly requests it.
- No real unit names, insignia, flags, classification markings, coordinates, operational locations, or recognizable sensitive documents.
- No robots, androids, holograms, glowing command centers, neon cyber grids, dramatic explosions, aircraft glamour shots, or generic defense-marketing imagery.
- No invented documentary claims. These images are conceptual covers, not proof that an event occurred.
- No logos or watermarks.

## Production Notes

1. Generate at highest available resolution.
2. Produce at least four candidates per prompt.
3. Keep one candidate with maximum negative space and one with a denser artifact composition.
4. Do not ask the model to render headlines. Site typography should remain HTML/CSS.
5. For reference-assisted prompts, upload only sanitized artifacts cleared for public display.
6. Preserve real reference content where requested. Do not allow the model to rewrite labels, invent fields, or add military markings.

## Optional Variation Suffixes

Append one suffix at a time after a base prompt.

### More documentary

> Reduce stylization by 20 percent. Increase natural paper wear, imperfect alignment, subtle pencil dust, and believable handling marks. Keep the scene clean enough for publication.

### More architectural

> Increase geometric precision, negative space, clean planes, and raking light. Reduce small props. Make the composition feel like an architecture monograph.

### Darker operational version

> Shift 70 percent of the frame toward charcoal green and dark olive while preserving detail in the shadows. Keep lime use below 3 percent of the image area.

### Lighter editorial version

> Shift the background toward warm paper and muted stone. Use charcoal only for lines, binder cloth, and deep accents. Preserve strong contrast for dark text overlay.

### No-text recovery

> Remove every generated letter, number, symbol, pseudo-word, watermark, and logo. Replace them with blank space, simple lines, or abstract geometric blocks.
